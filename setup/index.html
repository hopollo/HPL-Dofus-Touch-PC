HPL - Dofus Touch PC

This app runs the official Dofus Touch client on your PC. It does not include any game file: pick your own Dofus Touch APK once, and the app takes what it needs from it.

Use the latest version: copy it from your Android device, or download it from a trusted APK site (.apk, .xapk and .apkm are accepted). Do the same after each game update, with Options ▸ Change APK.
